You have installed Claude Code, given it a first task, written a CLAUDE.md and picked a permission mode. Time to put the four lessons together on something real: a one-page landing page for a product or service you actually care about. No coding experience is needed. You describe, Claude builds, you review.

## The task

Produce a single `index.html` (styles may be inline or in one `styles.css`) that presents your product: a headline, a short pitch, three benefits, one call to action and a contact line. It has to open in a browser from your disk and look fine on a phone.

## Steps

1. Create an empty folder and start `claude` inside it.
2. Ask Claude to write a short `CLAUDE.md` first: what the product is, who the visitor is, the tone of voice, and that the page must stay a single HTML file without build tools.
3. Describe the page in one message. Be specific: the exact headline, the three benefits in your own words, the wording of the button.
4. Review the diff before accepting it. If a sentence does not sound like you, say so and ask for a rewrite of that sentence only.
5. Open the file in a browser, then narrow the window to phone width. Ask Claude to fix anything that overflows.
6. Run `/cost` and note how many tokens the whole exercise took.

## What to check

- The page opens from disk with no console errors.
- Every text block is about your product, not placeholder prose.
- Nothing is cut off at 360 px width.
- The CLAUDE.md you wrote would make sense to a colleague who joins the project tomorrow.
